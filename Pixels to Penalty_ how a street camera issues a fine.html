Pixels to Penalty

A traffic camera never sees a crime. It sees boxes of pixels, and everything after that is arithmetic. Tap any car to follow it from camera frame to fine. Weigh pads, not cameras, catch overloaded trucks.

Blue lines = speed trap (known distance). Orange = weigh pad. Violet = plate camera. Green boxes = what the camera thinks it sees.

Under the hood: break the system on purpose

Try: set the ruler error to +8% and watch honest drivers get fined (wrongful fines turn red). Drop the shutter to 1/125 and plates blur into look-alikes (8 and B, 5 and S), so more fines need a human. Untick interpolation at 4 fps and timing gets coarse. Set reckless drivers to 0% and nobody is fined.

Speed = known distance ÷ time between two lines. Red light = crossing the stop line while the signal log says red. Overload = weigh pad reading above the class limit (+5%). The tracker links detections frame to frame by predicted position. Plate reading confuses look-alikes with a chance that grows with motion blur (speed × exposure). A challan is issued automatically only on an exact registry match; near matches go to a human.
